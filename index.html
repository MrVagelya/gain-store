FAQ

        

        

          

            How do I buy?
            

              Click Buy on Discord, open a ticket or DM, and say which plan you
              want. We’ll send payment details and your key.
            

          

          

            Can I share my key with a friend?
            

              No. Keys are HWID-locked to one device. A second PC will be denied.
            

          

          

            What executors work?
            

              Any executor with HttpGet and loadstring (and optional
              syn.request for license checks). If yours fails, message us on Discord.
            

          

          

            I got “License denied”
            

              Double-check script_key, use the loader from this site, and make sure
              you’re not using a key already bound to another PC. Contact support with your key
              (DM only).
